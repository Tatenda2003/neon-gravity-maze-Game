Neon Gravity Maze

HOW TO PLAY

[ Left / Right Keys ] or [ A / D ] to Move laterally
[ Spacebar ] or [ W / Up ] to Toggle Gravity and Walk on Ceilings!

OBJECTIVE: Collect ALL glowing yellow coins to clear the maze!
(Avoid the green slime monsters!)
